Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and only collect what we need to process your order.

When you check out, we collect your name, email, contact number, delivery address, and payment details (your payment method, reference number, and a screenshot as proof of payment). We don't store your full bank or e-wallet account details, just what's needed to confirm your payment.

Your information is used only to process and deliver your order, verify payment, and get in touch with you about it. We share your name, address, and contact number with J&T Express so they can deliver your package, and we use your payment reference to confirm payment with GCash, Maya, or your bank. We never sell or share your information for marketing purposes.

We keep your order information only as long as needed to fulfill your order and handle any related concerns. If you'd like us to delete your information, just email us and we'll take care of it, unless we need to keep certain records for legitimate business reasons.

Our site may store things like your cart items in your browser so you don't lose them while shopping — that stays on your device and isn't sent to us unless you check out.
