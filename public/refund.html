Since every press-on set is made by hand just for you, we're not able to accept returns or exchanges for reasons like changing your mind or picking the wrong size once we've started making it.

That said, we'll make it right if:

  • You received the wrong item or design
  • Your set is defective

If any of these happen, email us (aureji2025@gmail.com) within 3 days of receiving your order with your order ID, a photo or video of the item, and a quick description of the issue, and we'll sort out a replacement, store credit, or refund.

If an item you ordered sells out after you've paid, we'll let you know and offer a substitute, store credit, or a full refund for that item. For booked nail services, please give us as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, refunds aren't given for services already done, unless there was a clear mistake on our end.

Approved refunds go back through the same payment method you used to pay. If you have any concerns about your order, just reach out — we're happy to help sort it out.
